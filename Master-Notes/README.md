## How to revise
1. Read the concept and why it matters.
2. Trace the small example and predict its output.
3. Review the ML connection and common mistakes.
4. Finish with the quick-revision bullets and practice prompts.

## Scope
The goal is to support classical ML and provide useful foundations for deep learning. For ML workflows, remember: split data first; fit learned preprocessing only on training data; apply the same fitted transformation to validation/test data.

**Style:** Simple English · Balanced explanations · Small and real-world examples · Tips, pitfalls and quick revision.
